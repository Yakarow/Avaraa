Questions, answered

Is this safe, especially for girls travelling solo?

Trips are hosted by Aishwarya, who is with the group the whole time. Groups stay small, and stays and activities are arranged by us. You can message her on WhatsApp before you book, with any question at all.

Can I come alone, or do I need to bring someone?

Come alone. Trips are designed so strangers become friends by the first evening.

How does payment work?

Pay ₹5,000 to block your slot, then the balance before the deadline. Booking is confirmed only after full payment.

What's the cancellation policy?

The advance is non-refundable in case of cancellation.

Do I need to pick a trip now, or can I just join the community?

You can just join. Pick "Not sure yet" in the form and we'll tell you first when new dates drop.
